Our Story

Founded in 1998, The Savory Table began as a small family bistro in the heart of the city. We believe that food is more than just sustenance; it's a way to connect people. Every dish we serve is prepared with passion and a commitment to locally sourced, sustainable ingredients.

Our kitchen is led by Chef Julian Rossi, who brings over 20 years of experience in Michelin-starred restaurants to your table.
